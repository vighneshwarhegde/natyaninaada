# About

#### My Artistic Journey

Art is not just a performance; it is a spiritual journey. For me, Bharathanatyam and Carnatic Sangeetha are two sides of the same divine coin. 

With years of dedicated practice under esteemed gurus, I have developed a style that respects tradition while embracing the joy of contemporary storytelling. My mission is to share the beauty of Indian classical arts with the world, making it accessible and inspiring for all ages.
